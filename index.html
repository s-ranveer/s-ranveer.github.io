02 / Research

Research

Building AI for consequential domains.

My research currently involves large language models, probabilistic models, relational learning, reinforcement learning, and AI for healthcare. I investigate the use of LLMs for downstream tasks across clinical domains, ranging from relational information extraction from electronic health records to evaluating the role of LLMs in causal reasoning to solving relational multi-agent problems.

01

Clinical AI with LLMs

Exploring whether LLMs can serve as approximate knowledge sources to support learning in clinical settings, which are often data-poor and developing methods and frameworks for evaluating and mitigating hallucination and stochastic behavior in clinical decision-support tasks.

02

Relational reinforcement learning

Studying multi-agent domains where states and actions are defined by objects and their relations, creating richer but exponentially larger problem spaces.
